Privacy Policy

Yalla Ludo Star Online • BlueAppBox Gaming
Last Updated: October 2026
Package Name : com.blueFirstLudo.mywebapp

Welcome to Yalla Ludo Star Online, developed and operated by BlueAppBox. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play our game.

1. Information Collection and Storage

We respect user confidentiality. Our application is designed to function with minimal data collection:

  • Local Gameplay Data: Your coin balance, match statistics, winning streak, trophies, sound preferences, player display name, and chosen avatar are stored locally on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage. This data remains on your phone and is not sold to third parties.
  • Custom Profile Picture & Name: If you select an avatar or upload an image from your device gallery, it is processed locally in the app's cache for your in-game profile representation.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our application displays advertisements to keep the game completely free to play. We integrate the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to serve Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process non-personally identifiable device information, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device model, operating system, and hardware specifications
  • Coarse, non-precise location and internet network state
  • Ad interaction metrics to prevent ad fraud and deliver relevant advertisements

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy: Google Advertising & Privacy.

3. Virtual Currency & Purchases

All virtual coins in Yalla Ludo Star are earned through gameplay or by watching optional rewarded video advertisements. Coins have no real-world monetary value, cannot be redeemed for real cash, and cannot be transferred outside the game.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Yalla Ludo Star Online is intended for general audiences and family-friendly entertainment. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can promptly take appropriate action.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

Because your career data is maintained locally in your phone's sandbox environment, you have full control over your data. You can erase all game records, custom avatars, and coin balances at any time by clearing the application storage via:

Android Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Yalla Ludo Star ➔ Storage ➔ Clear Data

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new game updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
